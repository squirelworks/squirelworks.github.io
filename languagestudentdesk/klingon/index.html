Why pIqaD matters

  • Authentic representation of the language
  • One-to-one sound-to-symbol mapping
  • The form used by the Klingon Language Institute (KLI)

Basic Grammar

Word Order: OVS

Klingon is Object-Verb-Subject. The object comes first, then the verb, then the subject.

Example: Duj legh HoD = "The captain sees the ship"

Verb Prefixes

  • jI- = I (no object)
  • bI- = you (no object)
  • vI- = I (object is him/her/it)

Useful Phrases

  • nuqneH — What do you want?
  • Qapla' — Success!
  • maj — Good.
  • ghobe' — No.
  • HIja' — Yes.
