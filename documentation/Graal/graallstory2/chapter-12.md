## Chapter Twelve — Beside, Not Over

She wrote the morning beside the other morning, and the clerk checked the placement before he checked the sentences.

Beside meant the second leaf stayed the second leaf. The first leaf, the harbor, stayed on the left. No line crossed from the new page onto the old one. Over would have been a revision. A revision would have been a sanding. She had promised not to sand. He put a weight on the left leaf, a small one, so a draft could not slide under it and call itself a correction.

“Training,” he said. “It does not need a world to be in the mood. That is why it can sit beside a finish without arguing with the finish.”

She went to the lanes.

Afternoon was the list of things the new morning was allowed to know.

The pacer touched the frame. One picture. The grass in the picture was the board’s grass, not the quay’s. She had taken the files out of the frame on the eighth day and had not put them back. The abstinence held. Abstinence is a dull virtue. Dull had been load-bearing for several mornings now. She was no longer insulted by it.

Evening, the weight came off the left leaf, and nothing had slid under it.

Two mornings. One a harbor that had finished her. One a lane that had not needed a world. Side by side. Not stacked. Stacking would have settled which day was the real day, and both were real, and they were not the same day. Graal kept its disagreements in pairs. She had met the shape in a file, and in a mouth, and now in her own handwriting. A pair is not a problem. A pair is a report that refused to collapse.

She did not number a thirteenth morning. Numbering would have been a vow, and a name for a shell had already been refused the status of a vow. If there was another day, it would sit to the right of this one, with its own weight, and it would not explain this one, and it would not sand the harbor in order to be kinder.

The bell rang. Nobody was standing over it.

The nail on the lane was null. The nail on the quay was unbuilt. Neither fact had been used to mend the other.

The length was still the length.

That was the accurate end of the day.
