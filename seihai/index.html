Seihai

Seihai is an open client and server suite for Graal, the top-down online adventure game. The client plays offline levels and connects to Graal Reborn listservers and gservers. The servers serve classic clients from 1.33 onward, and Seihai itself. The suite is written in Rust, apart from two GPL forks in C++ and the Delphi client rebuilds.

This page is a small browser demo of one level. It is not the Seihai client. To play for real, build seihai from its README.

Client

seihaiThe Seihai program. It opens a start window, plays offline levels, reads server lists and logs into gservers.Rust
seihai-coreThe shared library: protocol, assets, scripting (GS1 and GS2), the offline simulation, the version profiles for each client build, and the connection code. It draws nothing. Every other Rust repo pins one of its tags.Rust

Servers

seihai-gserverA new game server on seihai-core, for classic clients (1.33 to 2.31 first) and Seihai: logins for every protocol generation, shared levels and gmaps, files, weapons and board changes.Rust
seihai-npcserverThe NPC-server. It logs into seihai-gserver and runs server-side NPC scripts.Rust
seihai-listserverThe listserver. It gives clients, Seihai and RCs their server list, and lets gservers register and verify accounts.Rust
seihai-rcA Remote Control client for server staff: players, accounts, options, files and NPC Control.Rust
seihai-servertoolsA lab world in one command, a setup wizard for going public, a world checker, and the LAT level and gani editors.Rust

GPL forks

seihai-gserver-v2The Seihai fork of GServer-v2, with fixes for real 1.33 to 2.31 clients. It ships first, while the Rust server catches up.C++
seihai-gs2libThe Seihai fork of gs2lib, which seihai-gserver-v2 builds on.C++

The forks keep their GPL licence. No code from them goes into the Rust repos.

Research

Graal_analysisCleaned notes on Graal formats, the wire protocol, NPC scripting and level design, by client version. No raw decompiles, code or binary addresses.Python
graal-client-reproRebuilds of the classic Graal clients, one per build, in Delphi as the originals were. They are tested against the Seihai servers.Pascal

The demo

Four small levels of our own, in the Graal .nw text format: a village, a meadow to the east, an old wood to the south, and a house in the village. Walk off the edge of an outdoor level to go on to the next one, and walk in at the door to go inside. The tiles are Seihai's own original pics1, in Graal's tile layout.

It uses the classic client's keys. Arrows move. A reads signs, opens chests and talks. S swings the sword and cuts bushes. D lifts a bush or rock, and throws it. Tab opens chat. Esc closes a sign, or goes back to the desktop.
